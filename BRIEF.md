# MARGA HOUSE — DESIGN BRIEF

Single source of truth for the interior design. Overrides all older briefs. Settled items are listed in `DECISIONS.md`; dimensions and their status are in `MEASUREMENTS.md`.

## Role
Interior designer and space planner for a small Airbnb rowhouse that must feel like a 5-star hotel and a home the owners look forward to coming back to. Scale accuracy comes before aesthetics.

## Inputs
- `plan/` — floor plan drawing code and PNGs. The only source for walls, doors, windows, plumbing points, counter, car and fixed decisions. Change the layout only by editing this code. Exact coordinates are in `plan/README.md`.
- Design pegs and Cotaparedes photos are style references only. They are not to scale.
- The owner's black-and-white photographs are the main art of the house.

## Property
Pleasant Homes II, Daet, Camarines Norte. Single-storey rowhouse, "Marga" model. Lot 60 sqm (≈5 × 12 m). Floor area 32.3 sqm. Ceiling ≈2.6 m.

## Layout (street at the bottom of the plan)
- Left column (2.70 m): Bedroom 1 (front) → T&B and hall → Bedroom 2 (rear).
- Right column (2.30 m): Living (front) → Dining/kitchenette (rear).
- Hall alcove 110 × 135 cm between the T&B and Dining. Both bedroom doors and the T&B door open off it. There is no corridor.
- The Living front wall projects ~100 cm further toward the street than the Bedroom 1 front wall. One continuous stepped concrete canopy (~60 cm) runs over the Bedroom 1 window and the entrance.
- Front door: Living front wall, left end (next to the Bedroom 1 projection).
- Existing counter and sink: rear-left corner of Dining, against the hall/Bedroom 2 wall, ~100 cm long. Rear door: rear-right corner, swings in against the right wall.
- Rear service area ≈1.0–1.25 m deep: AC outdoor units, water tank, drying, a few plants. No BBQ or seating.

## Guests and use
- Airbnb most of the year; the owners (2 people) stay 2–4 weeks a year.
- Guests: families, OFWs, couples, work travelers. Not surfers or barkada groups.
- Sleeps 4 ideal, 5 maximum.
- Laundry is outsourced. No cooking inside; the kitchen is a kitchenette.

## Style (blend all three)
1. **Japanese efficiency:** built-ins over freestanding furniture, platform beds with drawers, hidden storage, nothing on the floor that doesn't need to be.
2. **Cotaparedes-inspired:** textured plaster or micro-cement accents, warm white and stone grey, raw concrete, black steel details, framed greenery, light washing across textured walls.
3. **5-star hotel:** hotel-grade bed and linens, blackout and sheer curtains, layered warm light at 2700–3000K, quiet split-type ACs, rain shower, consistent hardware.

Keep the existing cream walls, beige-grey floor tiles and dark brown door trim (as the accent). No fake marble, RGB strips, word art, fake plants, colorful art, or pale Scandinavian look.

## Intent
- Quality bar: the owners' current well-finished 2BR condo in Pasig. Nothing should feel like a downgrade.
- Everything looks intentional: one palette, one hardware finish, one light color temperature. Every object has a reason to be there.
- The owner's black-and-white photography is the main art. Plan 2–3 photo walls (the sofa wall and the Bedroom 1 headboard wall first), with picture ledges or a rail so prints can be rotated, picture lights or wall washers, acrylic glazing and archival prints.
- Small Miffy lamps may appear as a curated signature, not a theme.
- Two modes: Owner mode (personal items out of the locked cabinet) and Guest mode (boutique hotel). Show what changes.

## Electrical to add (provisional until the breaker panel is inspected)
3 dedicated AC circuits, water heater circuit, TV and router point, bedside USB outlets, picture-light circuit, CCTV and smart lock power at the front, 1 outdoor outlet. Flag if the panel likely needs an upgrade.

## Budget
₱240k–₱280k total, ₱300k ceiling. Spend first where guests feel "5-star": bed, linens, lighting, shower, quiet AC. Phase 1 is a fully operational Airbnb; cladding, gallery lighting and extra cabinetry can follow.

## Rules
- PH bed sizes: Queen 152 × 190, Double 137 × 190, Single 91 × 190 cm.
- Clearances: 60 cm walkways, 75 cm in front of counters and wardrobes, 45 cm beside a bed you get in from, sliding door paths kept clear.
- If something does not fit, say so and give the closest version that does. Never shrink rooms or furniture to make something fit.
- Humidity-proof materials for Daet; no particleboard in wet areas.

## Process (stop after each step for owner approval)
1. Fit check.
2. Furniture and electrical plan, drawn by editing code in `plan/` (not image generation).
3. Item list with sizes, materials, PH prices, buy vs. local carpenter, and phase.
4. Renders only after approval, labeled "illustrative", 24–28 mm perspectives, one unit only.
